Harmonized Burning Activity Calendar

Bridging MODIS and VIIRS for Consistent Fire Analytics

The Challenge

  • Sensor Discontinuity (MODIS vs. VIIRS)
  • Data Overload for Emergency Managers
  • Lack of intuitive historical context

Phase 1: Harmonization

Normalizing spatial resolution and detection sensitivity.

  • Cross-sensor calibration (2012-Present)
  • Statistical scaling of Fire Radiative Power (FRP)
  • Unified time-series (2000-Present)

Phase 2: Visual Analytics

Turning raw data into decision support.

  • Interactive Heatmap Calendars
  • Real-time anomaly detection
  • Downloadable CSV & Visual Reports

How it works

From raw detections
to one calendar.

3.9M
Detections in

Active-fire pixels from NASA FIRMS: MODIS Terra and Aqua, VIIRS S-NPP and NOAA-20.

3.1M
Kept

Low-confidence pixels dropped. Gas flares, plants and other static heat sources masked.

4,917
Grid cells

Counted per 0.25° cell and month, with every sensor’s own count stored side by side.

1
Series

Everything in MODIS equivalents: Terra ×2.05, then Terra + Aqua, then VIIRS ×0.306.
